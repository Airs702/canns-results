Main point

CANNs links mechanism to measurement. Standardized continuous-attractor models, Rust-accelerated simulation, and topology-aware analysis connect predicted ring and torus geometry to real neural recordings in one reproducible workflow.

01 · Mechanism → geometry

One backbone spans ring, torus, and grid codes

Translation-invariant recurrence preserves a localized activity bump while one or two neutral phase directions encode a continuous variable. Each row below links a local network pattern to its population-level geometry.

Analysis rule. Maximum homology dimension 1 tests a ring; dimension 2 tests the toroidal cavity. Shuffle nulls guard against finite-sampling artefacts. A tuning map alone does not establish topology.

02 · Reproducibility baselines

Canonical computations share one backend

Head-direction, grid, and place networks use the same velocity-modulated theta drive while respecting their task-specific geometry.

Theta phase organizes internal trajectories

Within each cycle, activity sweeps around a ring or across a twisted torus. In the place-cell model, geodesic coupling follows accessible T-maze corridors instead of crossing walls.

  • 100 ms cycles repeatedly advance and reset the active sequence.
  • Velocity modulation creates task-specific sweeps with a shared implementation.
  • At the T-maze junction, slowing and turning reorganize the sequence.

Hierarchical path integration yields multiscale codes

Five grid modules drive a shared 30 × 30 place-cell readout over a 5 × 5 m arena, producing oriented bands, module-specific grid scales, and localized fields from one velocity input.

Evidence boundary: these are fixed-seed, toolkit-author reproduction baselines within one shared backend—not independent confirmations of the original studies.

03 · Measured performance

Rust acceleration makes long experiments feasible

Performance-critical kernels are exposed through Rust/PyO3 while model definitions and experimental workflows remain in Python.

≈700×

Navigation kernel at 106 steps

0.27 s with the Rust implementation versus 192.8 s for the pure-Python reference under the reported single-thread protocol.

Reported protocol: single-thread CPU on Apple M2 Pro (16 GB); 54 datasets across nine categories, 200–10,000 points, and dimensions 2–6. See the paper and repository for the complete benchmark definitions.

04 · Prediction → test

Topology-aware analysis of neural recordings

The Attractor Structure Analyzer (ASA) turns population activity into an auditable workflow: spikes → smoothed rates → PCA point cloud → persistent homology and circular coordinates.

A real MEC population yields a decodable ring

Mean absolute circular decoding error for MEC session 26648_1 after global sign and phase alignment. One dominant H1 interval supports a ring-like state space, and the decoded coordinate follows the behavioral head-direction trace.

Topology-aware cell selection links two scales

Every selected module showed negative GridScore–CohoScore correlation (mean −0.508; median −0.546; one-sided Fisher-z p ≈ 1.09 × 10−8).

Current limits: H2 is expensive above approximately 5,000 points; band-cell classification and several preprocessing choices still require scientific judgment.

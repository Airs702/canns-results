02 — ASA · Band cells

Complete analysis of four band-cell populations

High-band candidates from a 4,096-unit virtual population were separated by wavelength and orientation. ASA then tested each population with time-domain persistent homology, circular-shift controls, and one-dimensional cohomology coordinates.

Time-TDA with 100 circular-shift controls

Green intervals are measured persistence; the grey envelope summarizes the shuffle null. All dimensions computed by the run are shown.
